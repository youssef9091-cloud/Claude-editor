## The performance map

Pitch: **↗** rises · **↘** drops · **→** level · **⤴** lifts at the end · **⤵** falls at the end.

**The shape:** a playful first half (the names, the plaques) → a slide down to the quietest moment (the war, energy 1) → a slow way back up (Borges) → the peak (Maradona and 2–1) → a calm, balanced landing → a warm close.
**Rules for the read:** no two neighbouring blocks share an energy level, the lowest point (9) comes just before the climb to the highest (11–12), and the father's voice lives in the low-mid range, so his rises are what the ear notices.
