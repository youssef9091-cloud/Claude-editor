### 8 · بابا — the trigger · energy 2 · 0:37–0:48

- **Delivery:** a documentary narrator, with no drama in the voice. The cause-and-effect carries it.
- **Visual:** a small grey patrol-ship doodle circling the islands sails off the top of the frame on «سحبت», label «١٩٨١».
